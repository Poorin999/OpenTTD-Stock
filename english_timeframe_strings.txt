STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_5M                                             :{BLACK}Show one candle per 5 game minutes (5 real seconds). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_1H                                             :{BLACK}Show one candle per game hour (1 real minute). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_4H                                             :{BLACK}Show one candle per 4 game hours (4 real minutes). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_12H                                            :{BLACK}Show one candle per 12 game hours (12 real minutes). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_D1                                             :{BLACK}Show one candle per game day (24 game hours = 24 real minutes). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_W1                                             :{BLACK}Show one candle per game week (7 game days = 168 real minutes). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_MN                                             :{BLACK}Show one candle per game month (30 game days = 720 real minutes). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_TIP_TF_Y1                                             :{BLACK}Show one candle per game year (365 game days = 8,760 real minutes). Drag the chart with the mouse to scroll through the history
STR_STOCK_FRAME_WAIT                                            :{WHITE}Collecting candles. They appear as game time passes
STR_STOCK_TIP_CHART                                             :{BLACK}Mouse wheel: zoom in/out. Ctrl + mouse wheel: zoom the price. Drag: scroll in time (left/right; drag to the left past the newest candle to show the empty space of the future) and in price (up/down). Double click: reset the view
